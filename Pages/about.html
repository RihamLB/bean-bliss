About Bean & Bliss

Brewing happiness since 2026

Our Story

Bean & Bliss was born from a simple passion: to create a warm, welcoming space where coffee lovers and cookie enthusiasts could find their perfect match. What started as a dream in 2026 has blossomed into Rabat's favorite local café.

We believe that every cup of coffee tells a story, and every cookie brings a smile. Our mission is to serve not just beverages and treats, but moments of joy and connection in our community.

From sourcing the finest coffee beans to baking our signature cookies fresh daily, we're committed to quality in everything we do. Whether you're grabbing a quick espresso on your way to work or settling in with a latte and our famous caramel cookies, we want Bean & Bliss to feel like your home away from home.

Our Mission

At Bean & Bliss, we're on a mission to elevate the everyday coffee experience. We strive to:

Serve Excellence: Every drink is crafted with precision and care by our expert baristas using premium ingredients and time-honored techniques.

Create Community: We're more than a café—we're a gathering place where neighbors become friends and strangers become regulars.

Innovate Tradition: While we respect classic coffee culture, we're not afraid to experiment with new flavors and techniques to surprise and delight our customers.
